# Account deletion and data erasure requests

## Two different requests
Deleting a workspace removes the workspace and its files. A data erasure request, for example under GDPR, asks for personal data to be erased from our systems as well. Customers often ask for both together. Treat any request that includes erasure of personal data as a privacy case.

## Deletion is irreversible
Once deletion completes, files cannot be recovered. A workspace goes into a 14 day pending state first, during which an owner can cancel. Make sure the customer understands this and has exported anything they need, using Settings, Export workspace.

## Who can request it
Only a workspace owner can delete a workspace. For personal data erasure, the identity of the requester must be verified through the email on the account. Do not act on a request from an unverified address.

## What support does
Support never deletes data directly. Record the request, confirm what the customer wants removed and escalate to the privacy team, which handles verification, legal retention rules and the timeline. Billing records may be kept for as long as tax law requires.

## What to tell the customer
Say that the request has been passed to the privacy team, that they will be contacted at the account email, and that the legal response time is up to 30 days. Do not promise a date earlier than that.
