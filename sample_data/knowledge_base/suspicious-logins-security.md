# Suspicious logins and suspected account compromise

## Treat it as a security incident
A report of sign-ins the customer does not recognise, unexpected password resets or a possible breach is a security case. It is always escalated to the security team, whatever the plan. Support does not investigate access logs, confirm or deny a breach, or promise outcomes.

## Immediate steps for the customer
Ask the customer to change their password, sign out all sessions from Settings, Security, Active sessions, and enable multi-factor authentication if it is off. If an API key may be exposed, they should revoke it and create a new one. Workspace admins can also review the member list for accounts they do not recognise.

## What to collect
Record the time and approximate location of the sign-ins the customer saw, the affected user emails and whether any data was changed or shared. Keep the summary factual. Do not include passwords, codes or full keys in the ticket.

## What not to say
Never state that the account was or was not breached, and never describe internal detection methods. Say that the security team will review the activity and contact the customer through the verified email on the account.

## After escalation
The security team may temporarily lock the account to stop further access. Tell the customer that this can happen and that they will be told how to regain access.
