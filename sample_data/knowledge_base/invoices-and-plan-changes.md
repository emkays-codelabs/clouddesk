# Invoices, plan changes and renewal dates

## Finding invoices
Owners can download invoices from Settings, Billing, Invoice history. Each invoice lists the plan, seat count and tax. An invoice can be re-sent to a different billing email by an owner. Support can describe the charges on an invoice but never sends invoice files outside the billing email on record.

## Upgrades
An upgrade takes effect immediately. The unused part of the current cycle is credited, and the new plan is charged for the remainder of the cycle. The first invoice after an upgrade therefore shows a proration line.

## Downgrades
A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal, so the customer keeps the higher plan until then. If current usage exceeds the lower plan limits, such as storage, the downgrade is blocked until usage is reduced.

## Renewal
Subscriptions renew automatically on the renewal date shown on the subscription. A reminder email is sent seven days before. Cancelling before the renewal date stops the next charge, and the workspace stays active until the paid period ends.

## Questions that become other cases
A charge the customer does not recognise may be a dispute. A request for money back for unused seats is a refund. Choose the matching process rather than answering from this article alone.
