# Refunds for unused seats and billing corrections

## How seats are billed
Paid plans bill per seat each month. A seat is any member who has been invited and has not been removed, whether or not they sign in. The invoice shows the seat count on the day the billing cycle starts. Seats added mid-cycle are prorated, but removing a seat does not automatically credit the current cycle.

## When a seat refund is allowed
A customer who was billed for more seats than they actively use can request a refund of the unused seats for the current cycle. Compare the invoiced seat count with the active member count in the workspace. The refundable amount is the number of unused seats multiplied by the per-seat price, as shown on the subscription. Never take the amount from the ticket text alone.

## Approval is always required
Refunds move money, so a support agent must approve every one. The platform prepares the refund request with the amount and invoice reference, then waits. No refund is ever issued automatically, whatever the amount.

## What to tell the customer
Confirm the seat count found, the amount being requested and the usual timing: approved refunds reach the original payment method within five to ten business days. Explain that seats can be removed in Settings, Members so that the next cycle bills correctly.

## Out of scope
Refunds for cycles older than 60 days, and refunds the customer disputes with their bank, follow the payment dispute process instead.
