# Storage quota exceeded and upload failures

## What happens at the limit
Every plan includes a storage quota, measured across all files, versions and the trash. When usage reaches the quota, new uploads fail with "Storage quota exceeded". Existing files stay readable and downloadable. Nothing is deleted automatically.

## Check current usage
The subscription shows the quota and the amount used. The workspace admin can see a breakdown by folder in Settings, Storage. Compare the two figures before suggesting a fix, so the customer knows how far over or close to the limit they are.

## Freeing space
The quickest wins are emptying the trash, which still counts toward usage, and removing old file versions. Large archives and duplicated exports are usually the biggest items. Files deleted from the trash cannot be recovered, so advise the admin to check before they empty it.

## Upgrading
A plan upgrade raises the quota immediately and prorates the price for the rest of the cycle. Extra storage packs can also be added to Pro and Business plans without changing seats. Explain the options and let the customer choose; do not push a plan change.

## Grace period
Workspaces that go over by a small margin get a 48 hour grace period before uploads are blocked. If a customer asks for a short extension while they clean up, escalate to billing with the workspace name and the date they expect to be under quota.
