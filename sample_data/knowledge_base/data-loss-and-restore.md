# Missing files and data restore requests

## Start with the simple causes
Files that seem to vanish are most often moved, deleted by a teammate or hidden by a filter. Ask the customer to search by file name across the whole workspace and to check the trash. Deleted items stay in the trash for 30 days and can be restored by the owner of the folder or a workspace admin.

## Version history
Each file keeps its earlier versions for 30 days on Pro and 90 days on Business and Enterprise. If a file was overwritten or edited by mistake, an earlier version can be restored from the file's History panel without any help from support.

## Activity log
Workspace admins can open the activity log to see who moved or deleted an item and when. This often settles the question without further work. Share what the log shows only with admins of that workspace.

## Real data loss
If the files are not in the trash, not in version history and the activity log shows no deletion, treat it as possible data loss. Do not promise recovery. Escalate to the data protection team with the workspace name, the folder path, the approximate time the files were last seen and what the customer has already tried.

## Setting expectations
Backups exist for disaster recovery and are restored by the data team only. Recovery from backups can take one to three business days and is not guaranteed.
