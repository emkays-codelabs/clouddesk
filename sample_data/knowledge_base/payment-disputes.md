# Unauthorized charges and payment disputes

## Recognise a dispute
A dispute is any report that a charge was not authorised, or any mention of a chargeback or a bank dispute. It is different from a refund request for seats they did not use. Disputes carry financial and legal consequences, so they are always escalated to the billing disputes team.

## Gather the facts
Ask for the invoice number or the date and amount of the charge, the last four digits of the card and the email on the account. Never ask for the full card number, the security code or a password. If the customer posts them, do not copy them into notes.

## Check the account
Look at the subscription and invoice history to see whether the charge matches a renewal, an upgrade or added seats. Share a plain description of what the charge covered, but do not conclude that it was valid or invalid; that is for the disputes team.

## What not to do
Do not issue a refund while a dispute is open, because a refund and a chargeback can pay the customer twice. Do not argue with the customer or suggest they withdraw the dispute with their bank.

## Escalation
Pass the case to the billing disputes team with the invoice details and a short summary. Tell the customer the team will reply within two business days and that no further charges will be taken while the matter is reviewed.
