# Sharing folders with people outside the workspace

## Why the share button is blocked
When a member cannot share a folder with an outside email address, the cause is nearly always a workspace policy. Admins can turn external sharing off for the whole workspace or restrict it to approved domains. Members cannot override this, which is intended.

## Where the setting lives
Admins open Settings, Sharing and check "Allow sharing with people outside this workspace". Below it, the allowed domain list limits guests to listed companies. A third control decides whether links need a sign-in or work for anyone who has them.

## Member roles
Only owners and admins can change sharing policy. Editors can share a folder externally only if the policy allows it and the folder is not marked confidential. Viewers can never share. Ask the customer which role the member has before suggesting any change.

## Guest access
Guests are free of charge and only see the folders shared with them. They can be given view, comment or edit rights and can be removed at any time from the folder's Share dialog. Remind admins to review guests every quarter.

## What support can and cannot do
Support can explain the policy and point to the setting. Support cannot change a workspace policy on the customer's behalf, because that is an admin decision. If the admin is unreachable, ask the customer to contact them directly.
